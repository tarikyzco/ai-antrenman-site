Delete account

Last updated: 08.10.2026

The Turkish text prevails. This is a translation of the Turkish draft. Türkçe

Delete in the application

Open Profile → Account → Delete the account. Type the confirmation word shown on screen (DELETE in the English interface), then tap Delete my account permanently. Account access ends and removal starts for workout history, sets, programmes, weekly plans, health and pain records, progress photos and any stored personal API key. This cannot be undone. Records subject to legal retention obligations may be excepted.

Subscription

Deleting the account or application does not cancel the App Store subscription. First cancel through iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → SETRAIN → Cancel Subscription.
