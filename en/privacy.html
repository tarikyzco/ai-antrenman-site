SETRAIN is a mobile application that lets you record your workouts and work with an artificial intelligence coach. This page explains what data we collect, why, with whom we share it and what controls you have.

Publisher: [YAYINCI AD SOYAD — to be completed] · [YAYINCI ADRES — to be completed] · [DESTEK EPOSTA — to be completed]

At a glance

  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or track you across applications.
  • Your health information, measurements and photos are processed only with your permission.
  • If you use the AI coach, data needed to generate a response is sent to an AI provider. You see which provider and approve it beforehand.
  • You can export your data or delete your account and all your data at any time.
  • The application is for adults aged 18 and over.

How long we keep data

For as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, your data is deleted within [SILME SURESI — to be completed]; backup copies are destroyed within [YEDEK SAKLAMA SURESI — to be completed]. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Each user's data is isolated from other users at database level. Your photos are held in private storage and shown to you only through short-lived signed links. No system is completely secure; in the event of a data breach, you will be informed as required by law.

Your controls

In the application, from Profile:

  • Export my data: receive a copy of all your data.
  • Delete my account: your account and data are permanently deleted. Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your App Store subscription; cancel it in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Privacy and Permissions: withdraw each permission separately.
  • Sign out of all devices.

If you signed in with Apple, the connection to Apple is also revoked when you delete your account.

Children

The application is not for people under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has opened an account, we delete the account and their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the new version here and notify you of significant changes in the application. If a consent-based operation changes, we ask for your permission again.
