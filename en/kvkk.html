7. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; request information if it is; learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate processing; request deletion or destruction under the conditions in Article 7; request notification of corrections and deletions to third-party recipients; object to an adverse outcome resulting from analysis exclusively by automated systems; and request compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

For processing based on explicit consent, you may withdraw consent at any time in Profile → Privacy and Permissions in the Application. Withdrawal takes effect prospectively.

8. Requests

In the Application you can directly export my data, delete my account and manage my permissions.

For other requests, under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Applications to the Data Controller, you may apply in writing to the address above, through the KEP address, or from your registered email to [KVKK BASVURU EPOSTA — to be completed]. Your request is resolved free of charge as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest. If it is rejected, the response is inadequate or no response is given on time, you may lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board.
